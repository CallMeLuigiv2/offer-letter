## Results

| What | Number |
|---|---|
| Kokoro, 3 lines (12.6 s of audio), incl. model load | 5.5 s |
| Render 480p15 | 12.8 s (≈ 1.0× real time) |
| Render 1080p60 (`-qh`) | 39.9 s (≈ 3.2× real time) |
| Lip-flap | works: frame-to-frame diffs of ~530 in the portrait's mouth area while speaking, 0 in pauses |

So a 90-second chapter is about 5 minutes at 1080p60 (roughly half at 1080p30) and about 1.5 minutes
at 480p15. Pre-rendering per ticket is fine; "your plan, played out" can render at 480p in the
background while he keeps planning.

## What it showed (fix before building the renderer)

1. **Backgrounds compete with diagrams.** The pixel office at 28% opacity is still busy behind the
   timeline (coherence principle). Explainer scenes need their own backgrounds (the real-life story's
   place: bakery, post office…) generated in the portraits' style, plus a darkened/blurred panel behind
   any diagram.
2. **Portraits carry their own studio backdrop.** Framed as a rectangle it looks pasted on. Either a
   deliberate card (rounded frame + name plate, VN-style) for narration, or cut-outs with alpha for
   characters acting inside the scene (needs a background-removal pass on the 84 portraits).
3. **Fonts and colours** should come from the studio theme (Catppuccin roles), not Manim defaults.
4. Lip-flap from loudness is enough at this size; expressions swap per beat (`expression` field).

Verdict: D13 is feasible on this PC with the existing studio; the renderer's real work is the story
widgets (Stage, Character card / cut-out, Prop) and the backgrounds.
